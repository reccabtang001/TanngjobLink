Common job application requirements

Documents

Updated CV, national ID details, education certificates and any documents explicitly requested by the employer.

Remote-work readiness

Reliable internet, a suitable device, communication skills, time management and role-specific digital skills.

Before you apply

Read eligibility rules, location restrictions, deadlines, salary terms and the official application process.

Scam alert: Never pay an unverified recruiter to guarantee a job. Confirm vacancies on the employer's official website and be careful with requests for passwords, PINs or one-time codes.
